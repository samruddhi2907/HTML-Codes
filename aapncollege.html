Sadguru Gadge Maharaj College (SGM College) in Karad, Maharashtra, was established in 1954 by Padmabhushan Dr. Karmaveer Bhaurao Patil. Affiliated with Shivaji University, this autonomous, NAAC 'A+' accredited campus spans 6.8 hectares and serves over 12,500 students across diverse arts, science, and commerce programs.
